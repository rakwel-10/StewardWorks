Results

Real support needs. Real solutions.

Process Improvement

Creating clarity, structure, and stronger systems

The challenge

A nonprofit undergoing restructuring needed to move from unclear roles and fragmented processes to a more organized operating structure with clearer responsibilities, stronger communication, and systems that could support a leaner staff.

The outcome

I stepped in as the contracted Community Organizer for seven months to bring structure, streamline processes, and strengthen the organization’s operating foundation.

  • Centralized external requests into a designated platform to improve visibility and coordination
  • Right-sized processes with staff to reduce unnecessary steps and improve efficiency
  • Updated the operations manual to better support a leaner staffing structure
  • Built an assessment tool to support ongoing quarterly review and process improvement
  • Identified opportunities to redirect funding toward higher-priority operational needs

Event & Project Management

Coordinating people, details, and outcomes

The challenge

A federal land management agency needed to deliver a seamless orientation experience for 200–250 incoming interns, requiring coordinated travel, lodging, communications, staffing, and event logistics.

The outcome

I served as the Project Manager to oversee the successful completion of the event.

  • Managed travel, lodging, participant communications, and day-of event logistics
  • Built and maintained administrative workflows and tracking systems to keep moving parts aligned
  • Coordinated planning across staff and stakeholders
  • Helped create a consistent framework for executing future orientation programs

Operations & Admin

Building capacity for sustainable growth

The challenge

A growing small business had reached a point of capacity. Critical administrative functions, including billing, scheduling, and office operations, needed structure and dedicated support to keep it moving forward.

The outcome

I stepped in as a fractional Operations & Administrative Consultant to close immediate capacity gaps while building the systems needed for sustainable growth.

  • Created clear operating SOPs to support the onboarding of a new administrative assistant
  • Took ownership of billing, scheduling, and office administration
  • Established processes that clarified day-to-day responsibilities and reduced operational gaps
  • Built a recruitment pipeline to support future staffing needs
  • Helped transition the organization from reactive administrative support to a more structured operating model
Common questions

Before you get in touch.

What kinds of organizations do you work with?

Nonprofit and mission-driven organizations, and the changemakers inside them: teams committed to lasting impact who need the structure to sustain it.

Can you step into a program that already exists?

Yes. Engagements run in both directions: building a new program from concept, or coming into an existing one to strengthen systems, manage a project, or provide operational support.
