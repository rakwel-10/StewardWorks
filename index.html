The approach

How the work moves.

Four stages, from a concept on paper to a program your team runs without you.

  1. 01

    Concept

    The idea gets developed or refined until it is specific enough to plan against — who it serves, what it delivers, what success looks like.

  2. 02

    Plan

    Capacity building strategy, budget, and timeline. Vendors and logistics mapped, risks named early rather than discovered late.

  3. 03

    Launch

    Implementation, with the launch toolkit alongside it — checklists, templates, and workflows your team uses from day one.

  4. 04

    Sustain

    Procedures written down, staff trained on them, and the program handed over in a state your team can carry forward.

Then it loops — review, document, improve.

A program is never finished on the day it launches. What gets reviewed and written down is what survives staff turnover, grant cycles, and growth — which is why process improvement runs alongside the work rather than after it.

Common questions

Before you get in touch.

What happens in the assessment?

The capacity assessment is where most engagements begin. It reads where your organization’s capacity is strongest and where the gaps sit, so the work that follows is aimed at the right problems. It runs on its own for $2,500, and it is built into the Foundations package.

What kinds of organizations do you work with?

Nonprofit and mission-driven organizations, and the changemakers inside them — teams committed to lasting impact who need the structure to sustain it.

Can you step into a program that already exists?

Yes. Engagements run in both directions — building a new program from concept, or coming into an existing one to strengthen systems, manage a project, or provide operational support.

What do we keep at the end?

Documented procedures, a program launch toolkit of checklists, templates and workflows, and a team trained to use them. The work is built to run without me.

Do you help with hiring?

Yes — strategic talent search and placement, informed by a decade of nonprofit program management and recruiting.

What does it cost?

Foundations runs $6,300–$8,400 across six to eight weeks. Growth & optimization runs $10,500–$12,600 across ten to twelve. Individual services are available à la carte, from $500 for a single SOP. Final pricing is quoted to your scope.
